# Cheap-but-Delicious Eats: Northern Taiwan + Nationwide Chains, Convenience Stores & Famous Drinks

Research date: 2026-10-03. Method note: WebFetch was blocked by the network egress proxy for guide.michelin.com, taiwanobsessed.com, taiwanderers.com, taipeitravelgeek.com and taipeitimes.com, so findings below come from search-result summaries of those and other pages (blogs, Tripadvisor, Yelp, Foursquare, Wikipedia, Focus Taiwan). Prices are as reported by the cited source and its date; Taiwan street-food prices have been rising (2024–2026), so treat every price as "approximately, at time of source". **No coordinates were verified. ## Q1. Nationwide cheap chains worth trying: what to order + typical NT$ price

### Takeaway
The core "Bafang-style" cheap, famous, everywhere chains are Bafang Yunji (dumplings), Formosa Chang (braised pork rice), Two Peck and Hot-Star (giant fried chicken cutlets), Mos Burger (rice burger), the breakfast chains Mei Er Mei and My Warm Day, and bubble-tea chains (50 Lan, Milksha, CoCo, Xing Fu Tang, Tiger Sugar, Chun Shui Tang). Dumplings and lu rou fan meals run about NT$60–150. Fried chicken cutlets cost about NT$65–95. Mos rice-burger combos are NT$180–220.

### Cited Findings

**Bafang Yunji 八方雲集 (Bafang Dumpling)**
- Founded 1998; source cites 777 stores in Taiwan plus Hong Kong/China locations; "in nearly every neighborhood"; potstickers (guotie) are the main dish with several flavors, fried or steamed, "usually costing NT$6 to NT$10 per dumpling" — [Taiwanderers chain guide (via search summary)](https://taiwanderers.com/taiwan-chain-restaurants-cafes/); also see [Wikipedia: Bafang Dumpling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bafang_Dumpling)
- Example Taipei branch: No. 56, Sec. 2, Hankou St., Wanhua (Ximending area) — [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g13806951-d10191401-Reviews-Ba_Fang_Yun_Ji_Dumplings-Wanhua_Taipei.html)

**Formosa Chang 鬍鬚張 (lu rou fan chain)**
- Started as a small food stand in Taipei in 1960 and grew into a nationwide chain; signature dish is braised/minced pork rice (lu rou fan); also serves soups, chicken and hot pots; described as a cheap option — [Wikipedia: Formosa Chang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formosa_Chang); [Taiwanderers (via search)](https://taiwanderers.com/taiwan-chain-restaurants-cafes/)

**Two Peck 2派克 (crispy fried chicken cutlet chain)**
- Breaded fried chicken cutlet about NT$60–65 per piece (sources differ). Reported as NT$10 cheaper than Hot-Star at some locations. Over 360 locations in Taiwan. Takeaway stalls with no seating — [Alvinology](https://alvinology.com/2008/10/03/two-peck-fried-chicken/) / [My Inner Fatty](http://www.myinnerfatty.com/2010/06/two-peck-fried-chicken.html) (search summary; both are old posts, so the prices are likely out of date)
- Taipei branch example: Nanyang (Zhongzheng District, near Taipei Main Station cram-school street) — [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g13808853-d19929040-Reviews-Two_Peck_Crispy_Chicken_Nanyang-Zhongzheng_District_Taipei.html)

**Hot-Star Large Fried Chicken 豪大大雞排**
- Born 1992; cutlets "at least 30 cm" — [Hot-Star (Canada site) About](http://www.hot-star.ca/about.html)
- Original/famous location: Shilin Night Market area, No. 115 Jihe Rd (基河路115號), Shilin — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/%E8%B1%AA%E5%A4%A7%E5%A4%A7%E9%9B%9E%E6%8E%92-%E5%A3%AB%E6%9E%97%E5%8D%80); Ximending branch at 漢中街121-1號, Wanhua (Yelp listing updated Sept 2026, so presumably still trading) — [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/%E8%B1%AA%E5%A4%A7%E5%A4%A7%E9%9B%9E%E6%8E%92-%E8%90%AC%E8%8F%AF%E5%8D%80)
- PRICE CONFLICT: one source reports about NT$70 as a "2025 estimate" ([MakeThatDish](https://makethatdish.com/hot-star-large-fried-chicken-taipei/)). Another reports NT$90 for the jumbo cutlet, rising to NT$95 as of June 2026 ([TaiwanMerch Shilin guide](https://taiwanmerch.co/culture/shilin-night-market-taiwan-guide/)). The newer NT$90–95 figure is more likely current.

**Mos Burger 摩斯漢堡 (Taiwan menu)**
- Signature is the rice burger (bun made of rice mixed with barley and millet). Combo examples: Yakiniku Rice Burger set NT$185, Seafood Rice Burger set NT$190, Shogayaki Rice Burger set NT$180, Beef Rice Burger with Egg set NT$220. Early-breakfast Seafood Rice Burger NT$125 — [everymenuprices (updated Sept 2026)](https://everymenuprices.com/mos-burger-menu-with-prices/); delivery-menu listings at [Uber Eats Taipei](https://www.ubereats.com/tw-en/store/%E6%91%A9%E6%96%AF%E6%BC%A2%E5%A0%A1mos-burger-%E5%8C%97%E5%AE%89%E5%BA%97/2sixl-YoXVGcb4CKdVLkXg). (Aggregator prices, so verify; delivery menus usually cost more than in-store.)

**Breakfast chains: Mei Er Mei 美而美 / My Warm Day 麥味登 (also Laya Burger 拉亞漢堡)**
- Mei Er Mei is considered the first "Western-style" Taiwanese breakfast shop of its kind, with branches everywhere. My Warm Day has over 1,000 locations across Taiwan. Touristy breakfast shops usually have an English menu if you ask — [Taiwan Obsessed breakfast guide (via search)](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/taiwanese-breakfast/); [The Taipei Edit: Inside Taiwan's "Western" Breakfast Shops](https://thetaipeiedit.substack.com/p/inside-taiwans-western-breakfast)
- Laya Burger is another major breakfast chain — [Wikipedia: Laya Burger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laya_Burger); background on the "Western breakfast" shop phenomenon — [CommonWealth: How Taiwanese Breakfast Stopped Being Taiwanese](https://english.cw.com.tw/article/article.action?id=3801)
- Typical orders (danbing 蛋餅 egg crepe, toast sandwiches, iron-plate noodles, milk tea) and prices: NOT verified in this round. Taiwan Obsessed has a menu decoder at the link above.

**Bubble-tea chains (nationwide)**
- 50 Lan 五十嵐: 200+ locations; customizable sugar/ice; the origin of KOI (Singapore). Tip: "50% sugar and 70% ice is usually optimal" — [Traveloka](https://www.traveloka.com/en-sg/explore/culinary/exploring-taiwans-top-bubble-tea-acc/350347); [Lemon8 brand ratings](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@duckkie08/7399243083566629393?region=sg)
- Milksha 迷客夏: uses fresh milk instead of powdered creamer; order Taro Milk with Pearls — [Traveloka](https://www.traveloka.com/en-sg/explore/culinary/exploring-taiwans-top-bubble-tea-acc/350347)
- Xing Fu Tang 幸福堂: brown-sugar pearls stir-fried in front of you; order Brown Sugar Bubble Milk — [Traveloka](https://www.traveloka.com/en-sg/explore/culinary/exploring-taiwans-top-bubble-tea-acc/350347); [Wikipedia: Xing Fu Tang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xing_Fu_Tang)
- Tiger Sugar 老虎堂: "tiger stripes" of caramelized brown sugar on the cup — [Traveloka](https://www.traveloka.com/en-sg/explore/culinary/exploring-taiwans-top-bubble-tea-acc/350347)
- CoCo 都可: everywhere; one reviewer rates the taste "average" (6.5/10) — [Lemon8](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@duckkie08/7399243083566629393?region=sg)
- The Alley 鹿角巷: signature Brown Sugar Deerioca Fresh Milk; in Ximending you can compare 50 Lan, Tiger Sugar and The Alley back-to-back — [TaiwanMerch boba guide](https://taiwanmerch.co/culture/taiwan-boba-tea-guide/)
- Chun Shui Tang 春水堂: see Q3 (origin claim). Sit-down teahouse, so more expensive than takeaway chains.
- Chain bubble-tea prices: no reliable 2025–26 source found (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- For an app, Bafang, Formosa Chang, Two Peck/Hot-Star, 50 Lan/Milksha and Mei Er Mei/My Warm Day are the safest "cheap and everywhere" picks. They are "famous, cheap, delicious" in the same way Bafang is.
- Bafang's quoted "NT$6–10 per dumpling" comes from an undated blog. Treat it as a floor; a 10-potsticker order is plausibly about NT$60–90 now. Unverified.

### Gaps
- Official current menus and prices for Bafang, Formosa Chang, 50 Lan, Milksha and CoCo were not retrieved (official sites were not fetched; the egress proxy blocked several domains).
- 雙全 (shuang quan), Mister Donut Taiwan and Dante Coffee were not researched. No sources were gathered.
- Yonghe soy milk chains (永和豆漿): not researched beyond Fuhang (Q4).

## Q2. 7-Eleven & FamilyMart must-tries (with prices)

### Takeaway
The must-tries are tea eggs (about NT$13–15), roasted sweet potato (NT$30–60 by weight), oden (from about NT$20/piece), FamilyMart's Famichiki (about NT$99 reported) and seasonal soft serve (about NT$50), Kuai Kuai snacks (about NT$25), and bottled milks: Uni-President papaya milk, Lin Feng Ying 林鳳營 fresh milk and Kuang Chuan 光泉 soy milk.

### Cited Findings
- FamilyMart raised tea-egg prices to NT$13 from Dec 28, 2022 — [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202212190023)
- Tea eggs NT$13–15. Roasted sweet potatoes NT$30–60 by weight from a warming cabinet, "genuinely excellent in winter". FamilyMart seasonal soft serve "worth the NT$50" — [Taipei Tourism: Taiwan Convenience Stores](https://www.taipeitourism.org/taiwan-convenience-stores/)
- Oden (關東煮) from about NT$20/piece; 3 pieces about NT$60–80. Famichiki about NT$99 and FamilyMart-exclusive. Kuai Kuai (coconut puffed-corn snack) about NT$25. 7-Eleven has about 7,021 stores, FamilyMart about 4,251. FamilyMart is known for own-brand desserts and soft serve, 7-Eleven for ready meals — [Taipei Tourism: Taiwan Convenience Stores (search summary)](https://www.taipeitourism.org/taiwan-convenience-stores/)
  - NOTE: the NT$99 Famichiki figure looks high for Taiwan and may be confused with another product or market. Flag as unverified.
- Kuai Kuai cultural note: Taiwanese put it on servers and machines as a "behave" good-luck charm — [Taiwan Merch: Kuai Kuai](https://taiwanmerch.co/culture/daily-kuai-kuai-2026-07-30/)
- More 7-Eleven ideas (list articles) — [Klook: 14 Taiwan 7-Eleven goodies](https://www.klook.com/en-AU/blog/taiwan-7-eleven-goodies/); [Ms Travel Solo: Taiwan 7-11 food top 10](https://www.mstravelsolo.com/taiwan-7-11-food/); [Hop On World: FamilyMart Taiwan](https://hoponworld.com/taiwan-family-mart-food/); [Tiffy Cooks: full meal under US$5](https://tiffycooks.com/taiwan-convenience-store-full-meal-under-5-dollars/)
- Bottled drinks: the most common papaya milk is made by Uni-President (統一) — [Wikipedia: Papaya milk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papaya_milk). Kuang Chuan (光泉) bottled soy milk comes sweet or unsweetened. Lin Feng Ying (林鳳營) is a Wei Chuan (味全) brand and a household name. Wei Chuan, Uni-President and Kuang Chuan account for about 70% of local dairy processing — [Taipei Times milk guide (2019)](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/feat/archives/2019/08/24/2003721022); [Taiwan Merch: Taiwan Drinks](https://taiwanmerch.co/culture/taiwan-drinks/)

### Inferences
- Bento (便當), onigiri and 7-Eleven ready meals are must-tries, but prices were not captured in this round.

### Gaps
- Exact 2026 prices for bento, Uni-President papaya milk and Famichiki are not verified.
- Whether the 7-Eleven tea egg price matches FamilyMart's NT$13 was not confirmed.

## Q3. Famous drinks + where to get them in the north

### Takeaway
Bubble tea's invention is disputed between Chun Shui Tang (Taichung, says 1987) and Hanlin Tea Room (Tainan, says 1986). A court ruled in 2019 that the dispute was moot because bubble tea is not patentable. In Taipei, the brown-sugar milk chains (Xing Fu Tang, Tiger Sugar, The Alley) can be compared in Ximending. Papaya milk, winter melon tea and sugarcane juice are classic old-school drinks, but no specific northern shops were verified for these in this round. The famous brown-sugar "frog milk" shop Chen San Ding at Gongguan is CLOSED.

### Cited Findings
- Hanlin's claim: founder Tu Tsung-ho, 1986, after seeing white tapioca balls at a Tainan market. Chun Shui Tang's claim: employee Lin Hsiu-hui, 1987, mixed tapioca balls into iced milk tea — [Taipei Times: Who invented bubble tea? (2018)](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/feat/archives/2018/11/13/2003704115)
- In 2019, after a 10-year lawsuit, the court ruled it "irrelevant" who invented it because bubble tea is not a patented product — [Wikipedia: Bubble tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubble_tea) (via search summary); background at [Marketplace (2024)](https://www.marketplace.org/story/2024/03/14/bubble-tea-economic-survival-national-identity-and-betrayal)
- Chun Shui Tang in Taipei: "Try the Original Bubble Tea at Chun Shui Tang" — [Taipei Travel Geek](https://www.taipeitravelgeek.com/chun-shui-tang). The specific branch, address and price could not be fetched (domain blocked).
- **Chen San Ding 陳三鼎 (brown-sugar "frog" pearl fresh milk, Gongguan): CLOSED.** Yelp lists 陳三鼎青蛙鮮奶 at 羅斯福路三段316巷8弄2號 as "CLOSED" (page updated Sept 2026) — [Yelp](https://m.yelp.com/biz/%E9%99%B3%E4%B8%89%E9%BC%8E%E9%9D%92%E8%9B%99%E9%AE%AE%E5%A5%B6-%E4%B8%AD%E6%AD%A3%E5%8D%80). Foursquare marks the 創始店 (founding store) "Now Closed" — [Foursquare](https://foursquare.com/v/%E9%99%B3%E4%B8%89%E9%BC%8E%E9%BB%91%E7%B3%96%E9%9D%92%E8%9B%99%E9%AE%AE%E5%A5%B6%E5%89%B5%E5%A7%8B%E5%BA%97/4b9f4913f964a520f91a37e3). Taiwan News reported it was rumored to have closed after a cockroach incident and tax-evasion allegations, with the owner seen moving equipment out — [Taiwan News](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/3824155). No evidence of a relocated reopening was found.
- Papaya milk dates to the 1970s, made from milk (>50%) plus concentrated papaya juice. Convenience-store versions are sweeter and strained — [Wikipedia: Papaya milk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papaya_milk); [CET: papaya milk](https://cetacademicprograms.com/move-over-chocolate-milk-taiwanese-papaya-milk-is-the-flavor-of-the-summer/)
- Winter melon tea (冬瓜茶) is made from winter melon boiled with sugar. Winter melon tea and sugarcane juice (甘蔗汁) are caffeine-free old-school classics "what locals drank before chains existed" — [Wikipedia: Winter melon punch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_melon_punch); [TaiwanMerch boba guide](https://taiwanmerch.co/culture/taiwan-boba-tea-guide/)
- Overviews of Taiwanese drinks (for app copy) — [Taipei Travel: Drinkipedia (TAIPEI Quarterly 2020)](https://www.travel.taipei/en/pictorial/article/22754); [Taiwan Panorama: Taiwan's Cooling Beverages](https://www.taiwan-panorama.com/en/Articles/Details?Guid=876f0156-38e4-42cc-aa1b-0b2510d9d287&CatId=10&postname=Taiwan%27s+Cooling+Beverages); [Taiwan News: winter melon & chrysanthemum tea](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/5894590)

### Inferences
- In the app, list brown-sugar milk as a "drink category" pointing to Xing Fu Tang / Tiger Sugar / The Alley, not to Chen San Ding.
- Hanlin is Tainan-based and Chun Shui Tang is Taichung-based. Both have Taipei branches per general knowledge, but specific branches are unverified.

### Gaps
- No verified specific northern shop for winter melon tea, herbal jelly (仙草), sugarcane juice or Herb Alley (青草巷, Wanhua). The search returned only general articles.
- Chun Shui Tang and Hanlin Taipei branch addresses and prices are not verified.

## Q4. Specific famous cheap shops: Taipei / New Taipei / Keelung / Yilan

### Takeaway
About 30 well-documented cheap northern spots are listed below. Many are Michelin Bib Gourmand night-market stalls. Michelin's 2025 Taiwan Bib list had 144 entries, with Taipei leading at 37, and Su Lai Chuan 蘇來傳 (ba-wan and taro cake) newly added. Most items cost NT$30–100. Confirmed closure: Chen San Ding (see Q3).

### Inferences
- Combined with Q1–Q3 (about 14 chain entries and about 8 convenience-store items), these 29 place entries give about 50 app rows. Chen San Ding should be excluded or shown as "closed".
- Night-market stall hours follow the market (roughly 17:00–24:00) unless stated. Specific closed days were verified only for Fuhang (Mon), Lan Jia (Mon), Liu Yu Zi (Thu) and Rong's Pork Liver (Mon).
- Older blog prices (Jin Feng 30/50, Lan Jia 60, Fuzhou bun 50–60) are likely 10–20% low for 2026. Show them as "about" with a "prices rising" disclaimer.
