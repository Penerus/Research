## Q1. Master spot list: names, location, coordinates, classic shot, timing

### Takeaway
There are 42 candidate spots across 14 areas. The most reliably famous are Gaomei Wetlands, Sun Moon Lake, Alishan, Lotus Pond, Fo Guang Shan, the Dome of Light, Taroko/Qingshui Cliff, Mr. Brown Avenue, Sanxiantai, the Twin-Heart Stone Weir and Matsu's Blue Tears. Several famous Taroko sub-spots are still closed in 2026. For those, the app needs status flags rather than plain listings.

### Cited Findings
**Spot-level facts that have sources**
- **Gaomei Wetlands (高美濕地), Qingshui District, Taichung**: wind turbines run by Taiwan Power Company make it a popular sunset spot. Commons has a category for it, and a file titled "Wind power-Kaumei.jpg" is described as "Sunset of wind turbines from Kaomei Wetland" — [Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wind_power-Kaumei.jpg); [Commons category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Gaomei_Wetlands); ✔ [Wikipedia: Gaomei Wetlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaomei_Wetlands). Further files surfaced: "File:Sunset of Gaomei Wetland.JPG" — [link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Sunset_of_Gaomei_Wetland.JPG); "File:Gaomei Wetland sunset DSC 5441-2.jpg" — [link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Gaomei_Wetland_sunset_DSC_5441-2.jpg); "File:Taichung City Gaomei Wetland.jpg" — [link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Taichung_City_Gaomei_Wetland.jpg).
- **Rainbow Village (彩虹眷村), Taichung**: in mid-2022 workers painted over roughly 50% of Huang Yung-fu's murals. The site was repainted and reopened in 2023/2024 (sources differ on the year). Entry is free, hours are Tue–Sun 09:00–17:00, and it is closed Mondays — [Taiwan Obsessed](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/rainbow-village-taichung/); [TaiwanPlus: reopens](https://www.taiwanplus.com/news/taiwan-news/arts-and-entertainment/230602002/taichungs-rainbow-village-reopens); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/travel/article/rainbow-village-taiwan-intl-hnk/index.html); ✔ [Wikipedia: Rainbow Village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainbow_Village). Conflict: one snippet says it "reopened June 2023" and another says repainting "took two years… reopened 2024" — [Third Eye Traveller](https://thirdeyetraveller.com/rainbow-village-taichung/).
- **Alishan (阿里山), Chiayi County**: from Zhushan Observation Platform (2,451 m) you watch the sea of clouds and the sunrise over Jade Mountain. One early sunrise train runs each day, timed to sunrise, and tickets must be bought a day ahead. The 2026 cherry-blossom season ran Mar 10–Apr 10, with Yoshino cherries peaking mid-March and blossoms lining the Zhaoping Line — [Tripool Alishan guide](https://www.tripool.app/en/articles/taiwan-chiayi-alishan-attractions); [Nickkembel](https://www.nickkembel.com/alishan-taiwan-sunrise/); [Taiwanderers sunrise train](https://taiwanderers.com/alishan-sunrise-train-guide/).
- **Fenqihu (奮起湖)**: the full-line Alishan train makes a 30-minute stop at Fenqihu — [search summary of Foreigners in Taiwan / Girl on a Zebra](https://www.foreignersintaiwan.com/blog/alishan-forest-railway).
- **Taiping Suspension Bridge (太平雲梯), Meishan, Chiayi**: it sits about 1,000 m above sea level, is 281 m long, and the address is No. 5 Xiakengzi, Taiping Village, Meishan Township — [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g13806740-d12910020-Reviews-Taiping_Suspension_Bridge-Meishan_Chiayi_County.html); [Taiwan Obsessed](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/taiping-suspension-bridge-chiayi/).
- **Jingzaijiao Tile-Paved Salt Fields (井仔腳瓦盤鹽田), Beimen, Tainan**: Beimen's first salt fields and the oldest tile-paved salt fields still in existence. Open 09:00–18:00 daily, free, tel +886-6-7861629 — [twtainan.net (official)](https://www.twtainan.net/en/attractions/detail/4642); [Trip.com](https://www.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/tainan/jingzaijiao-tile-paved-salt-fields-13509123). The sunset shot over the shallow pans is the well-known one; see the Nickkembel photos at [Nickkembel Qigu](https://www.nickkembel.com/qigu-salt-mountain-tainan-jingzijiao-wapan-salt-fields-taiwan/).
- **Beimen Crystal Church (北門水晶教堂)**: a glass, pyramid-shaped church in the Beimen Wedding Beauty Land park. Open 09:30–17:30 Mon–Fri and 09:30–18:30 Sat–Sun — [search snippet aggregating Trip.com/mindtrip](https://mindtrip.ai/attraction/beimen-taiwan/jingzaijiao-tile-paved-salt-fields/at-AP600m3D) (aggregator only; verify).
- **Sicao Green Tunnel (四草綠色隧道), Annan, Tainan**: a boat ride through a mangrove tunnel. Open 08:00–16:00 daily. Tickets NT$200 full, NT$100 child or concession, NT$30 for the "Black-faced Spoonbill" ticket — [twtainan.net (official)](https://www.twtainan.net/en/attractions/detail/4506).
- **Lotus Pond (蓮池潭) and Dragon and Tiger Pagodas (龍虎塔), Zuoying, Kaohsiung**: the pond is open 24 h and free. The pagodas were closed for restoration. Their interiors reopened 14 Mar 2025, with a full reopening reported as 13 Apr 2025 (two dates given). Hours 06:00–17:30, free — [Trip.com](https://us.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/kaohsiung/dragon-and-tiger-pagodas-92979/); [blog showing they were closed](https://cavinteo.blogspot.com/2025/12/dragon-and-tiger-pagodas-closed-kaohsiung-city.html); ✔ [Wikipedia: Lotus Pond, Kaohsiung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotus_Pond,_Kaohsiung). ⚠ Conflict: a blog post dated Dec 2025 is titled "They Were Closed Though", so the pagodas may have closed again or the closure may have been temporary. Recheck.
- **Dome of Light (光之穹頂), Formosa Boulevard MRT station, Kaohsiung**: it is free to see inside the station. Light shows are listed at 11:00, 15:00 and 20:00 — [KKday blog via search](https://www.kkday.com/en/blog/60812/asia-taiwan-kaohsiung-things-to-do-2) (show times unverified against Kaohsiung Metro).
- **Fo Guang Shan Buddha Museum (佛光山佛陀紀念館), Dashu, Kaohsiung**: No. 1 Tongling Rd. Open 09:00–18:00 Mon and Wed–Fri. On Sat–Sun the Front Hall is open 09:00–19:00 and the Main Hall 09:00–18:00. Closed Tuesdays, free entry — [Tourism Administration](https://eng.taiwan.net.tw/m1.aspx?sNo=0002121&id=A12-00163); [official site](https://www.fgsbmc.org.tw/en/index.aspx).
- **Xiao Liuqiu (小琉球), Pingtung**: the only way there is the ferry from Donggang. Vase Rock (花瓶岩) is the island's icon, and Vase Rock beach is a top spot for snorkelling with sea turtles — [Nickkembel](https://www.nickkembel.com/xiaoliuqiu-lambai-island-taiwan/); ✔ [Wikipedia: Vase Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vase_Rock).
- **Qingshui Cliff (清水斷崖), Hualien**: the main viewpoint, Chongde Recreation Area, reopened 1 Jul 2025. The Huide viewpoint is closed while its platform is fully rebuilt. Highway 9 is fully open — [Taiwan Obsessed closed list (mid-2026)](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/closed-attractions/); [Foreigners in Taiwan](https://www.foreignersintaiwan.com/blog/qingshui-cliffs).
- **Liyu Lake (鯉魚潭), Hualien**: the largest freshwater lake in Hualien County, at the foot of Liyu Mountain — [search snippet, islandlifetaiwan](https://www.islandlifetaiwan.com/the-best-of-hualien/).
- **Mr. Brown Avenue (伯朗大道), Chishang, Taitung**: a straight road through rice paddies with the Central and Coastal Ranges behind it and no utility poles. The rice is golden late May–June and late Oct–Nov. The best light is 05:30–07:30, before the tour groups arrive — [we123go guide](https://we123go.com/article/taiwan-taitung-mrbrownnavenue-a2026guidetotaiwansmosticonicricefields); [Trip.com](https://www.trip.com/moments/poi-brown-boulevard-10762726/).
- **Duoliang Station (多良車站), Taimali, Taitung**: an elevated platform on the hillside facing the Pacific. Trains no longer stop there — [Trip.com](https://us.trip.com/moments/poi-duoliang-station-13492970/).
- **Jialulan (加路蘭) and Sanxiantai (三仙台), Taitung coast**: both are on the standard East Coast route, along with Fushan Fishing Reserve and Jinzun — [Trip.com search summary](https://www.trip.com/moments/poi-chishang-10520720/).
- **Twin-Heart Stone Weir (雙心石滬), Qimei, Penghu**: a double heart-shaped fish trap made of basalt and coral, about 700 years old, and Penghu's most photographed icon. The full double heart only shows as the tide goes out, so shoot between high and low tide — [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/taiwan/taiwans-islands/outer-islands/attractions/twin-hearts-stone-weir/a/poi-sig/1618822/357563); [PENGHU-GO (official)](https://www.penghu-go.tw/penghugo/index.php?action=brands_detail&br_uid=2078).
- **Green Island (綠島)**: Zhaori Hot Spring (朝日溫泉) is one of only three saltwater hot springs in the world and faces the sunrise. Sleeping Beauty Rock is best seen from the Great Little Great Wall (小長城) — [Taiwan Obsessed Green Island](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/green-island-taiwan/).
- **Kinmen, Shanhou Folk Culture Village (山后民俗文化村)**: 18 Fujian-style houses of the Wang family, built around 1900, considered Kinmen's best-restored old village — [Tourism Administration](https://eng.taiwan.net.tw/m1.aspx?sNo=0002126&id=R101).
- **Matsu, Dahan Stronghold (大漢據點), Nangan**: built 1975–76, with 430 m of tunnels. Renovated for tourism in 2006 — [Tourism Administration](https://eng.taiwan.net.tw/m1.aspx?sNo=0002127&id=A12-00249).
- **Matsu Blue Tears (藍眼淚)**: visible Apr–Sep, peaking Apr–Jun with May the best month. Viewing is best after midnight, between low and high tide, on dark, calm nights. Qinbi Village beach on Beigan is a recommended spot — [Taiwan News first sighting](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/6093131); [KKday Matsu guide](https://www.kkday.com/en/destination/tw-matzu); [Occasional Traveller](https://theoccasionaltraveller.com/blue-tears-matsu-islands-taiwan/).
- **Penghu International Fireworks Festival 2026**: ran 4 May–25 Aug 2026. Shows were Mon and Thu through 25 Jun, then Tuesdays 30 Jun–25 Aug, about 9 pm, with 33 shows plus trial shows on 20 and 27 Apr. 2026 had a Dragon Ball Z tie-in — [PENGHU-GO (official)](https://www.penghu-go.tw/penghugo/index.php?action=news_detail&br_uid=2897&web_lang=en-us); [Twosevenths](https://www.twosevenths.com/en/info/penghu-fireworks-basic/); [ICRT](https://www.icrt.com.tw/info_details.php?mlevel1=6&mlevel2=12&news_id=294983).
- **Hehuanshan (合歡山) and Cingjing (清境)**: summer is the season for the Cingjing grasslands and for stargazing on Hehuanshan. The area has a Dark Sky Park, and the Wuling Pass lookout is at 3,275 m — [Taiwan Obsessed Hehuanshan](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/hehuanshan-sunrise-hiking-snow/).

## Q2. Getting there: public transport, fees, hours

### Takeaway
The trunk routes are well documented: Taiwan Tourist Shuttle 6670 for Sun Moon Lake, 7322 or the Alishan Forest Railway for Alishan, East Taiwan buses 8161/8163/8165/8166 for Chishang, and Dingdong 8132/8135/8136/8158 for Duoliang. Hehuanshan (6658A, three runs a day; 6506 suspended) and Jingzaijiao (bus 61, two runs a day on certain days only) need explicit "limited service" warnings.

### Cited Findings
- **Sun Moon Lake**: Tourist Shuttle 6670 leaves from Taichung Gancheng Station roughly every 30 min, 07:20–19:45, and takes about 2 h. It has variants A–F. B and F are faster but run only until 16:45 — [Taiwanderers](https://taiwanderers.com/how-to-get-to-sun-moon-lake/); [Nantou Fun Bus (official shuttle page)](https://funbus.welcometw.com/en/blog/26); [iBus route 6670](https://www.taiwanbus.tw/eBUSPage/Query/QueryResult.aspx?rno=66700&rn=1704185822477&lan=E).
- **Alishan by bus**: Tourist Shuttle 7322 takes about 2.5 h — [Taiwanderers](https://taiwanderers.com/how-to-get-to-sun-moon-lake/) (route origin is Chiayi; check the departure points). Recreation-area entry is NT$300 per adult, or NT$150 with a bus receipt or for students — [Nickkembel](https://www.nickkembel.com/alishan-taiwan-sunrise/).
- **Alishan Forest Railway**: Shizilu–Alishan reopened 6 Jul 2024 after 15 years, so Chiayi–Alishan runs through again. Timetable from 10 Jan 2025: up trains Express No.1 at 09:00 and No.5 at 10:00 (arrives 14:56). Down trains No.8 at 11:50 (arrives Chiayi 15:45) and No.2 at 16:51. About 5 h each way, including a 30-min stop at Fenqihu. Sources conflict on frequency: some say "only one train per day" each way — [Foreigners in Taiwan](https://www.foreignersintaiwan.com/blog/alishan-forest-railway); [official AFR timetable](https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/EN/0000115); [Girl on a Zebra](https://girlonazebra.com/train-to-alishan-forest/).
- **Zhushan sunrise train**: NT$150 one way, NT$75 concession, NT$300 return (as of Mar 2025). Buy the day before — [Nickkembel](https://www.nickkembel.com/alishan-taiwan-sunrise/).
- **Hehuanshan**: Nantou bus 6658A from Cingjing runs only three times a day (08:20, 11:10, 14:30 up; 09:40, 12:30, 15:50 down). Bus 6506 to Songxuelou/Wuling has stopped running — [Taiwan Obsessed](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/hehuanshan-sunrise-hiking-snow/); [gojiakhong](https://www.gojiakhong.com/taiwan-transportation-hehuanshan/).
- **Jingzaijiao**: TRA to Xinying, then bus 61. It runs only twice a day each way, on Fri, Sat, Sun, Mon and holidays, and takes about 1 h 22 min. A car is recommended — [Nickkembel](https://www.nickkembel.com/qigu-salt-mountain-tainan-jingzijiao-wapan-salt-fields-taiwan/).
- **Mr. Brown Avenue**: East Taiwan Bus 8161/8163/8165/8166 to Chishang Station, then about 3.5 km on foot or by rented bike (Hwy 9 south, then Zhongdong 2nd Rd) — [Trip.com guide](https://www.trip.com/moments/detail/taitung-760-147387186/).
- **Duoliang Station**: Dingdong Bus (mountain line) 8132/8135/8136/8158 etc. from Taitung Bus Station to the "Duoliang Station" stop — [Trip.com guide](https://www.trip.com/moments/detail/taitung-760-147387186/).
- **Xiao Liuqiu**: ferry from Donggang only — [Nickkembel](https://www.nickkembel.com/xiaoliuqiu-lambai-island-taiwan/).
- **Kaohsiung and Tainan hours and fees**: see Q1 for Lotus Pond, Fo Guang Shan, the Dome of Light, Sicao, Jingzaijiao and the Crystal Church.

## Q3. Current status 2025–2026: closures and disaster damage

### Takeaway
As of 2026, Taroko is only partly open. Shakadang, Swallow Grotto, Baiyang and Zhuilu are still closed, Changchun Shrine is not expected back before 2029, and Buluowan and the Tunnel of Nine Turns were due to reopen "later in 2026". Qingshui Cliff's Chongde viewpoint is open, but Huide is still being rebuilt. In September 2025 the Matai'an barrier-lake flood hit Guangfu (central Hualien). The Alishan Forest Railway has run through to Alishan since July 2024.

### Cited Findings
- **Taroko, open in 2026**: visitor centre, Taroko Terrace Trail, Tianxiang Recreation Area, Dacingshui Recreation Area, Chongde Trail, Dali–Datong Trail, Liwushan and Cianyanlishan trails. The Lushui area partly reopened Feb 2026: the first ~250 m of Lushui Trail and the first ~450 m of the Lushui–Wenshan Trail — [Taipei Times, 6 Mar 2026 (via search snippet)](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/taiwan/archives/2026/03/06/2003853379); [MyTaiwanTour](https://www.mytaiwantour.com/news/detail/140-is-taroko-gorge-open-in-2026-yes-here-s-what-you-need-to-know-before-you-go).
- **Taroko, closed**: Buluowan Terrace and the Tunnel of Nine Turns were "to reopen later this year (2026)". Changchun (Eternal Spring) Shrine will not reopen until 2029 because of rockfall risk and insurance problems. Zhuilu Old Road is closed with no date — [Taipei Times, 6 Mar 2026](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/taiwan/archives/2026/03/06/2003853379). Swallow Grotto is fully closed, "will need many years". Shakadang Trail is closed, but visitors can stop on Shakadang Bridge. Baiyang Trail, its waterfall and the Water Curtain Cave are closed, "probably forever" per one blogger — [Taiwan Obsessed](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/is-taroko-gorge-open/); [Taiwan Travel Blog](https://taiwantravelblog.com/taroko-gorge-2025-current-status-post-earthquake/). (Quake magnitude is given as 7.2 by the Taipei Times snippet and 7.4 by bloggers. 7.2 is the CWA figure and 7.4 the USGS figure; the researcher's knowledge says both are in circulation.)
- **Qingshui Cliff**: Chongde reopened 1 Jul 2025. The Huide viewpoint and rest stop are closed for reconstruction as of mid-2026. Highway 9 is fully open — [Taiwan Obsessed closed list](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/closed-attractions/).
- **Guangfu / Matai'an flood**: the barrier lake formed after a landslide set off by Tropical Storm Wipha in July 2025. It overtopped on 23 Sep 2025 during Typhoon Ragasa's rains, sending about 60 million tonnes of water and debris into Guangfu. At least 19 people died (earlier reports said 14). The flood destroyed Mataian Bridge and flooded Guangfu railway station — [Focus Taiwan 23 Sep 2025](https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202509230029); [Taipei Times 24 Sep 2025](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2025/09/24/2003844324); [Taipei Times feature 2 Oct 2025](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/feat/archives/2025/10/02/2003844764). A **new** barrier lake on Matai'an Creek overflowed and flooded a village again on 13 Nov 2025 — [Focus Taiwan 13 Nov 2025](https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202511130009). ✔ [Wikipedia: Matai'an Wetland Ecological Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matai%27an_Wetland_Ecological_Park) (a former tourist wetland in the affected area).
- **Alishan Forest Railway**: runs the full line since 6 Jul 2024 — [Foreigners in Taiwan](https://www.foreignersintaiwan.com/blog/alishan-forest-railway).
- **Rainbow Village**: open in its repainted form, about half of it no longer original — [Taiwan Obsessed](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/rainbow-village-taichung/).
- **Dragon and Tiger Pagodas**: reopened Mar/Apr 2025, but a Dec 2025 blog reports them closed. Status is uncertain — see Q1.

### Inferences
- The app should mark Taroko as "partially open — check Taroko NP site before going" and point users to Qingshui Cliff (Chongde), Tianxiang, Dacingshui and Shakadang Bridge as the shots they can still get. Leave Swallow Grotto out, or list it as closed.
- Guangfu sits on the Hualien–Taitung (East Rift Valley) line between the Liyu Lake area and Ruisui. Even though Guangfu itself is not a photo spot, the app should warn that rail and road links through it may still be affected.

### Gaps
- No 2026 confirmation was found on whether Buluowan and the Tunnel of Nine Turns have actually reopened, or on Highway 8 traffic-control windows.
- No primary source was found on whether the East Rift Valley rail service through Guangfu is back to normal.
- No current status was found for Penghu, Green Island and Orchid Island facilities after the 2025 typhoons.
