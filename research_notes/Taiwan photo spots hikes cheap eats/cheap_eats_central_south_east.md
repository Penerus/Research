# Cheap-but-Famous Food Shops: Central, Southern and Eastern Taiwan (for a trip-planner app)

Research date: 2026-10-03. Method: web search of Michelin Guide pages, Focus Taiwan / Taiwan News, city tourism sites, and recent (2024–2026) Taiwanese food blogs. Note: guide.michelin.com, twtainan.net and several blogs were blocked for direct fetch from this environment, so many facts come from search-result snippets of those pages (cited by URL). Hours and prices in Taiwan change often (2023–2025 saw broad price rises); treat every hour/price as "last reported" and re-check on Google Maps before shipping.

**Coordinates policy:** No source fetched gave lat/lng. Coordinates below are rough estimates from the reported street address/landmark (3 decimals, roughly 100–300 m accuracy), marked `~approx`. They are NOT verified. Geocode each from the cited address before release.

## 1. Michelin Bib Gourmand context (2025 and 2026 lists)

### Takeaway
The 2026 Michelin Taiwan Bib Gourmand list (announced July 2026) has 146 entries: 23 Taichung, 30 Tainan, 21 Kaohsiung. Tainan added 3 new names and Kaohsiung added none. Several of the most famous tourist favourites (Wenzhang, Liu Qian, A-Cun beef soup, Du Hsiao Yueh, A-Tang congee) are **not** Bib Gourmand. Bib-listed beef soup in Tainan means Hsi Lo Tien, Shang Hao Chih and Shun Te.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 list: 146 Bib Gourmand restaurants, 13 of them new. 37 in Taipei, 23 Taichung, 30 Tainan, 21 Kaohsiung, 18 New Taipei, 10 Hsinchu County, 7 Hsinchu City — [Focus Taiwan, 2026-07-14](https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202607140017); [Taiwan News](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/6400668); [Michelin 2026 Bib article](https://guide.michelin.com/tw/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/taiwan-2026-bib-gourmand-list)
- New in 2026: Taichung added Don Moo (home-style duck). Tainan added BAA Wanli Goat, Chan Chi and Zhu-Ji Dong Tsai Ya (Zhu-Ji opened in 1942, BAA Wanli Goat in 1962). Kaohsiung had no new additions — [Taiwan News / Focus Taiwan via search summary](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/6400668)
- 2025 list: 144 Bib Gourmand, with 30 in Tainan, 24 in Kaohsiung and 23 in Taichung. Taichung's new 2025 entry was Kung Fu Shanghai Fish Balls (功夫上海手工魚丸) — [Michelin 2025 Bib article](https://guide.michelin.com/tw/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/michelin-guide-taiwan-2025-bib-gourmand-selection); [Taiwan News 2025](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/6177280)
- Bib Gourmand means three quality dishes (excluding drinks) for under NT$1,000 — [twtainan.net 2025 Bib news](https://twtainan.net/en/event/newsdetail/7694)
- Tainan Bib beef soups: **Hsi Lo Tien Beef Soup 西羅殿牛肉湯**, 98 Gongyuan S. Rd, North Dist. (broth simmered from bones, brisket, fat and onion, with thin lean slices added at serving) — [Michelin](https://guide.michelin.com/tw/en/tainan-region/tainan/restaurant/hsi-lo-tien-beef-soup). **Shang Hao Chih Beef Soup 尚好吃牛肉湯**, 6 Sec. 1 Beian Rd, North Dist., "often sells out before 11am" — [Michelin](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/tainan-region/tainan/restaurant/shang-hao-chih-beef-soup). **Shun Te Beef Soup 順德牛肉湯**, 59 Minquan Rd — [search summary of Michelin/twtainan](https://twtainan.net/en/event/newsdetail/7694)
- Tainan 2025 Bib also includes Yeh Family Squid Rice Noodles 葉家小卷米粉 (142 Sec. 2 Guohua St) and A-Hsing Congee 阿星鹹粥 (289 Sec. 3 Minzu Rd). A-Tang congee 阿堂鹹粥 and A-Cun beef soup 阿村牛肉湯 are not on the 2025 list. Wang's Fish Skin 王氏魚皮 is described as Bib-recommended — [TVBS Supertaste 2025 Tainan Bib map](https://supertaste.tvbs.com.tw/pack/355933); [pbear.tw 2025 Tainan Bib incl. removed list](https://pbear.tw/%E8%87%BA%E5%8D%97%E5%BF%85%E6%AF%94%E7%99%BB%E6%8E%A8%E4%BB%8B/); [albertblog Wang's](https://albertblog.tw/97505/wang-fish-skin)
- Kaohsiung Bib names include Mi Yuan Tzu Steamed Glutinous Rice, Chun Lan Gua Bao 春蘭割包, NIU LAO DA, Cianjin Braised Pork Rice 前金肉燥飯, Hou Chi Duck Rice, Bei Gang Tsai Rice Tube, Liang Chia Pig Knuckle, Hung Chi Rice Shop, Liao Chi Migao, Ciao Zai Tou Huang's Braised Pork Rice, Hu Dong Beef and others — [Michelin Kaohsiung Bib page (search summary)](https://guide.michelin.com/tw/en/kaohsiung-region/kaohsiung/restaurants/bib-gourmand)
- Taichung Bib names include Taichung Meatball (茂川肉丸, see below), A Kun Mian, Lao Shih Kuan Noodles, No Name Noodles, Ke Kou Beef Noodles, Fu Din Wang, Chin Chih Yuan, Shanghai Food Nantun, Kung Fu Shanghai Fish Ball and Don Moo — [Michelin Taichung Bib page (search summary)](https://guide.michelin.com/tw/en/taichung-region/restaurants/bib-gourmand)

### Inferences
- Because Kaohsiung had no new 2026 entries and its count fell from 24 to 21, three 2025 Kaohsiung Bibs were probably dropped in 2026. Which ones is unverified, so check the live Michelin page before tagging any Kaohsiung shop "Bib 2026".
- For the app, tag Bib status as "Bib 2025 (2026 status: check)" unless the 2026 page confirms it.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch the full 2026 per-city name lists (Michelin and taiwantable.com were blocked). So 2026 status for individual shops (e.g. whether Cianjin, Chun Lan, Maochuan, Hsi Lo Tien and Yeh's are still listed) is unconfirmed.

## 2. Tainan (core of the user's interest)

### Takeaway
Tainan is the best city for the user's taste. Beef soup is best at dawn (Liu Qian, A-Cun) or late night (Wenzhang). Pair it with Du Hsiao Yueh, milkfish congee, Chou's shrimp rolls, coffin bread, Anping tofu pudding and Shuang Quan black tea. Garden Night Market runs only Thu/Fri/Sat/Sun.

### Cited Findings (entries)

**T1. Wenzhang Beef Soup 文章牛肉湯 (Anping HQ)**: famous with tourists, open late, with a second branch at Dongning
- Address: 300 Anping Rd, Anping Dist. 06-358-7910. Hours 10:30–02:00, **closed Thursday**. Dongning flagship: 449 Dongning Rd, East Dist., 11:30–02:00, closed Thursday — [search summary of 2025 blogs: travel.yam, albertblog, dpm2bb](https://travel.yam.com/article/138067); [albertblog](https://albertblog.tw/85061/winchangbeef)
- CONFLICT: an older blog gives 590 Anping Rd, open 17:00 to 14:00 the next day, with a Monday 14:00 to Tuesday 17:00 break — [Irene's blog via search](https://ireneslife.com/blog/post/394467824). The 2025 sources list 300 Anping Rd, so treat 300 as current but re-verify.
- 2025 blog title says the Anping HQ offers free beef-broth refills and has a large dining room — [dpm2bb 2025](https://dpm2bb.com/2025-105/). Order: beef soup plus beef-mince rice (牛肉燥飯) — [rabbitfunaround](https://rabbitfunaround.com/blog/post/winchangbeef)

- Coordinates ~approx 22.998, 120.178

### Inferences
- For the app, Tainan has two time windows. The **dawn window** (04:00–11:00) covers Liu Qian, A-Cun, Wang's, A-Tang, A-Hsing and Shang Hao Chih. The **late window** covers Wenzhang (to 02:00), Shi Jing Jiu (evening) and Garden NM (Thu–Sun). Visitors arriving on a Tue–Wed cannot do Liu Qian or Garden NM.
- Wenzhang (closed Thu) and Liu Qian (closed Tue–Thu) mean a Thursday in Tainan misses both.

### Gaps
- No current per-bowl price for Wenzhang, Liu Qian, A-Cun, Hsi Lo Tien, Chou's or the coffin bread. Typical Tainan beef soup is roughly NT$100–200 per bowl, inferred from Shi Jing Jiu's NT$150 large bowl only.
- Bib 2026 status of each Tainan shop is unconfirmed.

## 3. Chiayi: turkey rice and Wenhua Rd Night Market

### Takeaway
Penshui Turkey Rice at the Central Fountain is the 70-year landmark. Guo Jia (inside Wenhua NM) runs almost round the clock. Lin Cong Ming fish-head casserole is the other must-eat.

### Cited Findings
- **C1. Penshui Turkey Rice 噴水雞肉飯 (HQ)**: 70+ years, five branches, the HQ sits beside the Fountain Circle. 325 Zhongshan Rd, West Dist. 05-222-2433. 09:30–21:30 — [search summary of 2025 Chiayi guides](https://www.housefeel.com.tw/article/%E9%9B%9E%E8%82%89%E9%A3%AF-%E7%81%AB%E9%9B%9E%E8%82%89%E9%A3%AF-%E5%98%89%E7%BE%A9%E9%9B%9E%E8%82%89%E9%A3%AF-%E5%98%89%E7%BE%A9%E9%9B%9E%E8%82%89%E9%A3%AF%E6%8E%A8%E8%96%A6/). Coordinates ~approx 23.480, 120.449. Gap: price.
- **C2. Guo Jia Chicken Rice 郭家雞肉飯**: 148 Wenhua Rd, East Dist. Daily 09:00–05:00. Called the "No.1 seat" of Wenhua NM food — [niniyeh](https://niniyeh.com/guojia-chicken-rice/); [search summary](https://travel.yam.com/article/136928)
- **C3. Lin Cong Ming Sand-Pot Fish Head 林聰明沙鍋魚頭**: 361 Zhongzheng Rd, East Dist. 11:20–21:00, **closed Tuesday** — [search summary](https://www.adamstartliving.com/post/chiayi-wenhua-night-market)
- **C4. Wenhua Rd Night Market 文化路夜市**: next to the Fountain Circle, nearly 24-hour. Stalls set up from about 15:00–16:00. Car-free pedestrian hours are about 19:00–23:00. Other picks: A-E Douhua 阿娥豆花 (60 years), A-Hsia chicken rice, Zhenzhen oyster omelette — [search summary](https://www.adamstartliving.com/post/chiayi-wenhua-night-market); [yam](https://travel.yam.com/article/136928)
