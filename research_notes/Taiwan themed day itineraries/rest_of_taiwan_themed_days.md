## Q1. What closures and fixed closed days must the app flag (as of 2026)?

### Takeaway
Taroko Gorge is only **partly** open. The headline trails (Shakadang, Swallow Grotto, Tunnel of Nine Turns, Baiyang, Eternal Spring Shrine, Zhuilu, Buluowan) were still closed through at least mid/late 2026. Highway 8 runs on timed release windows, and no public bus runs through the gorge to Tianxiang. Guangfu (south Hualien) is still recovering from the 23 Sept 2025 barrier-lake flood. Alishan's railway and branch lines are open, and the Mianyue Line trail reopened on 1 Sept 2026. Fixed weekly closures: Chimei Museum on Wednesdays; Fo Guang Shan Buddha Museum on Tuesdays; many museums on Mondays; Rainbow Village probably on Mondays (sources conflict).

### Cited Findings
**Taroko / Hualien**
- Taroko is "partially accessible, not fully reopened". It "has not returned to the experience travelers may remember from before the April 3, 2024 Hualien earthquake." Some spots reopened in early 2025, and more in summer 2025 and early 2026 — [Taiwan Obsessed: Is Taroko Gorge Open? (2026)](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/is-taroko-gorge-open/)
- Open per that page: Dekalun Trail (about a 1.5 h return hike behind the Visitor Center, steep with many stairs), the Taroko National Park Visitor Center, the Tianxiang trails and the Tabiduo Trail — [Taiwan Obsessed](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/is-taroko-gorge-open/)
- Still closed: Shakadang Trail, Eternal Spring Shrine Trail, Swallow Grotto, Tunnel of Nine Turns, Baiyang Trail and Zhuilu Old Trail. "A few will take several years to reopen and one or two may never reopen" — [Taiwan Obsessed](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/is-taroko-gorge-open/)
- Shakadang and Swallow Grotto "will need many years to recover", but visitors can stop on Shakadang Bridge and at the Swallow Grotto entrance for photos. Buluowan terrace and suspension bridge: "possible reopening in 2026", though the hotel "may never reopen". Tunnel of Nine Turns: "possible reopening in 2026" — [Just Taiwan Tour: Taroko Gorge Travel Tips 2026](https://www.justaiwantour.com/taroko-gorge-travel-tips-2026/); [Taroko Gorge: Essential Guide, Closures & Best Alternatives 2026](https://www.justaiwantour.com/places-to-go/taroko-gorge/)
- A Taipei Times report (March 2026) said Buluowan Terrace and the Tunnel of Nine Turns were scheduled to reopen later in 2026. Search summaries of later (July/Sept 2026) guides still list the Tunnel of Nine Turns as closed. Restoration status: 23 sites completed by end-2025 and 5 still under construction, including 4 tunnels due for repair by July 2028 — [Taipei Times, 2026-03-06](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/taiwan/archives/2026/03/06/2003853379); [Taiwan Obsessed: Taroko Gorge in 2026](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/taroko-gorge/). *The status in Oct 2026 is unclear. The app should say "check taroko.gov.tw".*
- One summary says "Trails within the Ecological Protection Area reopened on August 29, 2026, but this does not mean that the famous sightseeing trails inside Taroko Gorge have all reopened" — search summary of [Taiwan Obsessed](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/is-taroko-gorge-open/) / [Explorer Tom: Taroko National Park Guide 2026](https://explorertom.com/en/taroko-national-park-guide/). *I could not confirm which trails this covers.*
- Highway 8 "is not open 24 hours". Vehicles may pass only at "five designated release times each day" — [Just Taiwan Tour](https://www.justaiwantour.com/taroko-gorge-travel-tips-2026/). The release windows were given as 06:30–08:00, 10:00–10:05, 12:00–13:00, 15:00–15:05 and 17:00–18:00 — search summary of [we123go Taroko 2026 guide](https://we123go.com/article/abouttaiwan-TarokoGorge2026TravelGuide) / [Explorer Tom](https://explorertom.com/en/taroko-national-park-guide/). **This conflicts with** another guide, which says "Highway 8 is now reopened between 6.30am and 5.30pm daily" — [Taiwan Travel Blog: Taroko 2026 status](https://taiwantravelblog.com/taroko-gorge-2025-current-status-post-earthquake/). The National Park Service publishes the windows each month (e.g. "Traffic Control Measures for June 2026 on Provincial Highways 8, 14A, and 9") — [NPS Taiwan](https://www.taiwan.nps.gov.tw/home/en-us/news/33511.html) (could not be fetched).
- Buses: Bus 310 runs from Hualien railway station via Xincheng station to the Taroko National Park HQ visitor center. Bus 302 runs from Xincheng (Taroko) Station to the Visitor Center in about 8 minutes. "As of 2026, public bus service from the Visitor Center through the gorge to Tianxiang remains suspended" — [Taroko NP news on 302/310](https://www.taroko.gov.tw/en/News_Content.aspx?n=7880&sms=11222&s=297459); search summary of [Explorer Tom](https://explorertom.com/en/taroko-national-park-guide/)

Ruisui and Yuli are suggested for rafting, hot springs and cycling — search summaries of [Taiwan Travel Blog: Hualien besides Taroko](https://taiwantravelblog.com/what-to-see-and-do-in-hualien-besides-taroko-gorge/), [Nickkembel: 40 Things to Do in Hualien (2026)](https://www.nickkembel.com/things-to-do-in-hualien-taiwan/) and [Taiwanderers Hualien](https://taiwanderers.com/hualien-taiwan-things-to-do/)

**Guangfu flood (Hualien, East Rift Valley north)**
- On 23 Sept 2025 at about 14:50 the Matai'an Creek landslide dam failed after Typhoon Ragasa's rains. The water reached Guangfu Township around 16:00 — [Taipei Times 2025-09-24](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2025/09/24/2003844324); [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202509230029)
- Toll: 19 dead and 5 missing (later count). Guangfu has about 12,000 residents in 14 villages — [Focus Taiwan 2026-06-06](https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202606060013). Earlier reports said 14 and then 18 dead — [Taipei Times 2025-09-25](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2025/09/25/2003844383)
- Recovery: more than 450,000 volunteer person-times ("shovel heroes"), about 400,000 tonnes of mud removed, a steel bailey bridge reopened in January 2026, and 68 ha of farmland replanted — [GeoThings Guangfu Recovery](https://geothings.tw/usecases/guangfu/); [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202606060013). The legislature passed NT$27 bn for Hualien reconstruction in Dec 2025 — [Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/politics/202512020010)
- A "shovel heroes" commemorative exhibition opened in June 2026 at the **Hualien Tourism Sugar Factory** in Guangfu, a partnership of Rotary International and Taiwan Sugar Corp — [Focus Taiwan 2026-06-06](https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202606060013); [Taipei Times 2026-06-07](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/taiwan/archives/2026/06/07/2003858669)
- A new barrier lake caused evacuations in Nov 2025 — [Taipei Times 2025-11-13](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/taiwan/archives/2025/11/13/2003847157). A Taipei Times editorial in Sept 2026 ("Lessons from the Mataian disaster") shows the issue is still live — [Taipei Times 2026-09-25](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/editorials/archives/2026/09/25/2003864846)

## Q2. Taichung — themed day plans

### Takeaway
Taichung's strengths are an "arty/aesthetic" core (Calligraphy Greenway, Shenji New Village, Miyahara/Fourth Credit Cooperative), night-market food (Fengjia), indoor museums (NMNS and National Taichung Theater, both closed Mon) and a sunset finale at Gaomei Wetlands. For shopping, Mitsui Outlet Park Taichung Port and sun cakes are the anchors.

### Cited Findings
- The Calligraphy Greenway is a roughly 3 km walk linking CMP Block Museum of Arts to the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts, with shops and cafes. Shenji New Village is a former dormitory turned craft/creative quarter with handmade crafts and desserts, about 1 km+ from the Greenway — search summaries of [Differentville one-day Taichung](https://differentville.com/taichung-itinerary/), [The Nomadic Vegan](https://www.thenomadicvegan.com/taichung-itinerary-1-day-plus-2-3-days-options/) and [Agoda Shen Ji guide](https://www.agoda.com/travel-guides/taiwan/taichung/exploring-shen-ji-new-village-taichungs-hidden-arts-and-culture-oasis/)
- Miyahara is a restored former eye clinic turned dessert/ice-cream shop. The Fourth Credit Cooperative (Dawncake Group, in a 1966 credit-union building) is about 200 m away and sells similar products. Both open daily 10:00–21:00 — [Taichung Tourism: Fourth Credit Cooperative](https://www.taichung.travel/en/attractions/intro/1689); [Taiwanderers: Miyahara](https://taiwanderers.com/miyahara-taichung-ice-cream/)
- NMNS is closed Mon, Tue–Sat 09:00–17:00 — [Taichung Tourism NMNS](https://taichung.travel/en/attractions/intro/1691). National Taichung Theater is closed Mon — [Taiwan Obsessed Taichung](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/taichung-taiwan-guide/)
- Gaomei Wetlands: intertidal flat with wind turbines and a boardwalk; arrive about 1 h before sunset. Buses 178/179 run from Qingshui Station (20–25 min); the last bus back is around 18:30–19:30 depending on season — [ibeauty Gaomei guide](https://ibeauty.com.tw/gaomei-wetlands/); [KKday Gaomei guide](https://www.kkday.com/en/blog/63112/asia-taiwan-taichung-gaomei-wetlands-guide)
- Fengjia Night Market is the largest in Taichung. Peak hours are 19:00–22:00; arrive around 18:00 or on a weekday to avoid crowds — [rTaiwanR Fengjia guide](https://www.rtaiwanr.com/blog/taiwan-taichung-fengjia-nightmarket)
- Sun cakes originated in Taichung. Ruyi Sunny Cake is called "the undisputed king", having won the national sun-cake competition for years in a row. The Taiwan Sun Cake Museum is in a red-brick building, sells pastries and offers DIY workshops — [Tripadvisor Ruyi](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g13808534-d11947044-Reviews-Ruyi_Sunny_Cake_Fengjia_Taichung-Xitun_Taichung.html); [Oh Young souvenir guide](https://oh-young.academy/blogs/taichung-shopping/); [Wanderlog Sun Cake Museum](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/148190/taiwan-sun-cake-museum)
- Mitsui Outlet Park Taichung Port: Mon–Fri 11:00–21:30, Sat–Sun 10:30–21:30. From Taichung Station take bus 308 or 310 to Taichung Harbor Tourist Center (about 60 min), or 306/307 to Wuqi Elementary School (about 70 min). From Taichung HSR take bus 617 to Wuqi Elementary School. It is about a 12-min walk from either stop — [Mitsui Shopping Park official](https://mitsui-shopping-park.com/en/mop/special/taichungport/); [TaiwanFun](https://taiwanfun.com/mitsui-outlet-park-taichung-port/)
- Rainbow Village: free; its hours conflict (see Q1) — [Third Eye Traveller](https://thirdeyetraveller.com/rainbow-village-taichung/)

### Inferences (composed plans — times, durations and budgets are my estimates)
- **Shopping / aesthetic day:** 10:00 Miyahara + Fourth Credit Cooperative (1 h; buy pineapple cakes and ice cream) → walk or taxi to the Calligraphy Greenway / CMP Block (1.5 h) → 12:30 lunch nearby → 13:30 Shenji New Village (1.5 h; craft shops) → 15:30 bus or taxi to Mitsui Outlet Taichung Port (2–3 h; ferris wheel at sunset) *or* to Gaomei Wetlands (bus 178/179 from Qingshui) for sunset → 19:00 Fengjia Night Market (2 h). Budget about NT$800–1,500 excluding purchases (estimate). Wear walking shoes. Weekends are crowded at Shenji and Fengjia.
- **Photography (sunrise→sunset):** early Calligraphy Greenway → Rainbow Village (not Mon, unverified) → Miyahara interior → Shenji → Gaomei Wetlands sunset (the key shot). Check the tide.
- **Rainy day (not Mon):** NMNS (09:00, 2.5 h) → Miyahara/Fourth Credit Cooperative → National Taichung Theater (architecture) → a mall (Tiger City — no source data) → Fengjia (partly covered).
- **Food crawl:** Fourth Credit Cooperative dessert → sun-cake tasting/DIY (Taiwan Sun Cake Museum or Ruyi) → Fengjia Night Market. Bring cash.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on Tiger City (hours or access), Lecheng Temple, Taichung Second Market, Dakeng hiking trails or Taichung Park. The "Hiking/nature" and "Culture & temples" focuses for Taichung lack sourced stops (Dakeng trails are a well-known option but unverified here).
- No verified Taichung Station → Rainbow Village bus number.

## Q3. Sun Moon Lake & Cingjing / Hehuanshan — themed day plans

### Takeaway
Sun Moon Lake works as a classic sightseeing / photography / easy-cycling day: a boat loop of Shuishe → Xuanguang → Ita Thao piers, the ropeway to the Formosan Aboriginal Culture Village, and a bike path to Xiangshan. Cingjing/Hehuanshan is a high-altitude nature day: the Cingjing Farm sheep show, plus the Hehuan Main or East Peak easy summits off Hwy 14A, which has its own traffic controls.

### Cited Findings
- Suggested route: Shuishe Pier → lake cruise → Xuanguang Pier (temples) → Ita Thao Pier and Old Street → Ita Thao Trail → cruise back → cycle the lakeside "Water Bicycle Path" → Xiangshan Visitor Center. Boats run counter-clockwise, and a day ferry ticket allows one hop-off at each pier — [Asia Odyssey: Sun Moon Lake in one day](https://www.asiaodysseytravel.com/taiwan/sun-moon-lake-things-to-do.html); [Taiwan Obsessed Sun Moon Lake](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/sun-moon-lake-guide/)
- Ita Thao snacks: grilled wild boar sausage and handmade mochi; indigenous crafts. The lakeside trail leads to the ropeway station. The ropeway takes about 10 min each way over about 1,877 m to the Formosan Aboriginal Culture Village. Shuishe → Xiangshan cycling is about 1 h; e-bikes can be rented at Shuishe — [Will Fly for Food](https://www.willflyforfood.net/explore-sun-moon-lake-on-a-day-trip-from-taichung-taiwan/); [isp.tw Sun Moon Lake](https://www.isp.tw/sun-moon-lake/)
- Ropeway hours: weekdays 10:30–16:00, weekends 10:00–16:30, closed the 1st Wed monthly. FACV hours: weekdays 09:30–17:00, weekends 09:30–17:30 — [Trip.com Ropeway](https://in.trip.com/moments/poi-sun-moon-lake-ropeway-95636/); [Sun Moon Lake NSA FACV](https://www.sunmoonlake.gov.tw/en/consume/detail/611)
- Cingjing Farm is at about 1,700 m in Ren'ai Township. It has a sheep-shearing show and a sheepdog herding performance — [KKday Qingjing Farm](https://www.kkday.com/en-in/destination/tw-qingjing-farm); [Trip.com](https://us.trip.com/moments/poi-qingjing-farm-86072/)
- Bus **6658A** runs from Cingjing to Hehuanshan three times a day (08:20, 11:10, 14:30), returning 09:40, 12:30 and 15:50. A reservation is required — search summary of [Taiwan Obsessed: Getting to Cingjing](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/taichung-to-cingjing-farm/) / [Nantou Fun Bus](https://funbus.welcometw.com/en/blog/25)
- Hehuan Main Peak: trailhead between Kunyang and Wuling, about 1.8 km each way, about 1.5–2 h return, roughly 200 m gain. Park at Wuling Pass and walk about 15 min to the trailhead — [Taroko NP: Hehuan Main Peak](https://www.taroko.gov.tw/en/Tourism/TrailDetail?id=131); [Taiwan Obsessed Hehuanshan](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/hehuanshan-sunrise-hiking-snow/)
- Hehuan East Peak: from Songxue Lodge, about 300 m ascent and about 1 h to the summit, mostly on wooden stairs — [Taroko NP: East Peak](https://www.taroko.gov.tw/en/AttractionTrailContent.aspx?s=132&n=7900&sms=11273); [English in Taiwan](https://www.englishintaiwan.com/hikes/mt-hehuan-east-peak-hike)
- Wuling on Hwy 14A is the highest point reachable by road in Taiwan — [Taiwanese Secrets Hehuanshan](https://www.taiwanese-secrets.com/hehuanshan-taiwan/). NPS publishes monthly traffic controls covering Hwy 14A — [NPS](https://www.taiwan.nps.gov.tw/home/en-us/news/33511.html)
- A typical tour runs Taichung HSR → Cingjing Farm → viewing trails → sheep/equestrian show → Hehuanshan Dark Sky Park for stargazing — [Tripool Cingjing guide](https://www.tripool.app/en/articles/Cingjing-Farm-travel-guide-en)

### Inferences
- **Sightseeing classic (SML):** 09:00 Shuishe → boat to Xuanguang (temple) → 10:30 Ita Thao (lunch: boar sausage) → 12:30 ropeway to FACV (2–3 h; not 1st Wed) → back to Ita Thao → boat to Shuishe → 16:30 bike to Xiangshan for golden hour. Budget about NT$1,500–2,500 (boat pass + FACV/ropeway + bike + meals; my estimate, prices unverified).
- **Hiking/nature (Hehuanshan):** take the first 6658A at 08:20 from Cingjing → East Peak (1 h up) or Main Peak (2 h return) → return bus at 12:30 or 15:50 → Cingjing Farm sheep show in the afternoon. It is cold at about 3,000 m year-round: bring layers and rain gear. There is altitude-sickness risk if you come straight from the lowlands.
- **Photography:** SML sunrise from Shuishe or the lakeside path → Xiangshan → Hehuanshan Dark Sky Park for night skies (needs an overnight stay or a charter).
- **Relax:** SML cycling plus a lakeside café. **Rainy day:** FACV indoor sections, and Ita Thao shops.

### Gaps
- I did not verify SML boat-pass or FACV/ropeway combo prices, Nantou bus 6670 from Taichung, the sheep-show times, or the Wenwu Temple / Ci'en Pagoda hours.
- The Oct 2026 Hwy 14A traffic-control schedule is unverified.

## Q4. Chiayi & Alishan — themed day plans

### Takeaway
Chiayi city gives an easy culture/food/shopping day: Hinoki Village (daily 10:00–18:00), turkey rice and Wenhua Road Night Market. Turtle Island is 10.5 km off Toucheng; boats run Mar–Nov, with whale/dolphin peak in summer (May–Oct) — [Taiwan Obsessed Turtle Island](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/turtle-island-taiwan/); [Hoponworld](https://hoponworld.com/turtle-island-taiwan-guishan-island/)
