## Q0. 2026 closures, conditions and cross-cutting rules (read first)

### Takeaway
Key 2026 caveats: the Maokong Gondola had scheduled shutdowns (Apr 20–24 and Jun 8–28, 2026), Beitou's Millennium (public) Hot Spring has been closed for renovation since Jan 2025 with no reopening date, the Sandiaoling Waterfall Trail was reported closed/hazardous in spring 2026, and many Beitou and museum sights close on Mondays.

### Cited Findings
- Maokong Gondola closed Apr 20–24, 2026 (cable trimming, Section 2) and Jun 8–28, 2026 (annual major maintenance: drive wheel bearings, gearbox, main motor). Alternatives during closures: Brown 15 or S10 buses, or the Maokong Left Line tour bus. Phone (02) 2578-3536 — [CNA via washinmura mirror](https://aeo.washinmura.jp/ai/cna/en/news/2026-04-13-taipei-maokong-gondola-to-suspend-operat); [Taipei Times 2026-06-02](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/taiwan/archives/2026/06/02/2003858415); [Gondola FAQ](https://english.gondola.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=6E24B13638CFE055&s=C2D6B070D8711C62). The gondola also routinely closes for monthly maintenance (historic pattern of Monday closures) — not verified for 2026 in this search; check [english.gondola.taipei](https://english.gondola.taipei/).
- Sandiaoling Waterfall Trail: AllTrails user reviews (Apr–May 2026) report it closed / in poor condition — last ~0.5 mi "unmaintained, overgrown, slippery, unwalkable", multiple hikers falling — [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/taiwan/new-taipei-city/sandiaoling-waterfalls-trail). When open, tariff NT$60 full / NT$30 concession, six sessions 05:30–22:00 — [Nick Kembel Beitou guide (2026 info), via search snippet](https://www.nickkembel.com/beitou-hot-spring/); [Taipei Tourism Guide Beitou](https://www.taipeitourism.org/beitou-hot-springs-guide/).

## Q1. Hiking / Nature

### Takeaway
Best documented options: (A) Teapot Mountain + Jinguashi + Jiufen (bus 1062 / 965), (B) Yangmingshan (bus 260/R5 → 108) + Beitou soak + Elephant Mtn sunset is too much for one day; better split into Yangmingshan-morning/Beitou-afternoon. Caoling Historic Trail (Fulong↔Dali, 8.5 km) is open year-round and best Nov–Dec for silvergrass.

### Cited Findings
- Teapot Mountain: Keelung Bus 1062 from MRT Zhongxiao Fuxing Exit 2, alight at last stop Quanji Temple (main trailhead); or Bus 965 from MRT Fuzhong to "Gold Museum." Hike 3.5 km return, 305 m gain, 1.5–2 h; rock formations, coastal views — [The Sandy Feet Teapot Mtn](https://thesandyfeet.com/teapot-mountain-hike-taiwan-jiufen/); [Taiwan Hikes](https://www.taiwanhikes.com/blog-posts/teapot-banping-canguanliao-mountain.html); loop variant with Mt. Banping — [This Remote Corner](https://thisremotecorner.com/teapot-mountain-to-mount-banping-hike-near-jiufen/).
- Sample day: morning coffee in Houtong Cat Village → Jinguashi Gold Museum → hike (pack lunch) → evening in Jiufen → back to Taipei; or continue to Keelung Miaokou Night Market by any Keelung-bound city bus, which arrives at Keelung train station by the harbour/night market, then local train to Taipei — [This Remote Corner Jiufen](https://thisremotecorner.com/jiufen-an-easy-day-trip-from-taipei/); [Sandy Feet](https://thesandyfeet.com/teapot-mountain-hike-taiwan-jiufen/).
- Bus 1062 fare Zhongxiao Fuxing→Jiufen Old Street NT$105 full / NT$52 half (as of 2026-06), cash or EasyCard; ~every 15 min; Zhongxiao Fuxing is the starting terminal (best chance of seat) — [LalaLocker 1062 guide](https://www.lalalocker.com/blog/en/taiwan-jiufen-traffic/).
- Yangmingshan: Bus 260 from Taipei Main Station or R5/260 from MRT Jiantan to Yangmingshan Bus Terminal; transfer to 108 (park loop) for Xiaoyoukeng and Qingtiangang (Qingtiangang Loop Trail ~45 min); 108 runs ~07:00–17:30; Taipei→park 1.5–2 h — [Taiwan Obsessed Yangmingshan, via snippet](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/taipei-to-yangmingshan/); [Asia Odyssey](https://www.asiaodysseytravel.com/taiwan/taipei-to-yangmingshan.html).
- Common pairing: Yangmingshan morning, Beitou afternoon, Tamsui for sunset/dinner — [search summary of Beitou/Tamsui itineraries](https://therealtraveler.com/taiwan/beitou-hot-spring-yangminshan-guide).
- Elephant Mountain (Xiangshan): free; MRT Xiangshan (Xinyi/Red Line) Exit 2, follow signs for Lane 150, ~750 m walk, then ~500 steps to main viewpoint + ~100 more to top; arrive ≥30 min before sunset; sunset is significantly more crowded — [Taiwanderers](https://taiwanderers.com/elephant-mountain-taipei/); [Embrace Horizons](https://embracehorizons.com/elephant-mountain-hike-taipei/); [Taiwan Obsessed Elephant Mtn](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/elephant-mountain-xiangshan-taipei/).
- Caoling Historic Trail: 8.5 km between Yuanwangkeng (New Taipei) and Dali (Yilan); one-way or round trip Fulong↔Dali; open year-round, free, no permit; Nov–Dec silvergrass peak — [Tourism Administration](https://eng.taiwan.net.tw/m1.aspx?sNo=0002091&id=R114); [NE Coast NSA](https://www.necoast-nsa.gov.tw/Attraction-Content.aspx?a=268&l=2); [Take Your Backpack 2026](https://www.takeyourbackpack.com/backpacking-in-taiwan/hike-caoling-historic-trail/).
- Wulai: MRT Green Line to Xindian, then Bus 849 to Wulai terminus (~40 min, ~every 15 min) — [Love Taipei Wulai](https://lovetaipei.com/attractions/wulai-old-street/); [Jared's Detours](https://www.jaredsdetours.com/wulai-taiwan/).

### Inferences — Day plans
**Plan H1 "Gold Coast ridge": Teapot Mountain + Jinguashi + Jiufen** (moderate–strenuous; best on weekdays)
1. 07:30 Breakfast near MRT Zhongxiao Fuxing (convenience store or dan bing). 
2. 08:00 Bus 1062 from Zhongxiao Fuxing (Exit 2) → Quanji Temple last stop (~75–90 min, est.). Cash/EasyCard.
3. 09:30–12:00 Teapot Mountain (3.5 km return, 1.5–2 h; ropes/rock scramble at the "teapot" top — skip that part if wet). Pack water + snacks; little shade.
4. 12:15–13:30 Jinguashi: Gold Museum area, lunch (the "miner's lunch box" is a commonly cited option — unverified in this pass).
5. 14:00 Bus down to Jiufen (short hop; 1062/965 pass through — verify direction).
6. 14:15–16:30 Jiufen Old Street, A-Mei Teahouse viewpoint, taro balls. Expect peak crowds.
7. 17:00 Option: bus to Keelung (Miaokou Night Market, dinner) → TRA train to Taipei; or 1062 back (expect long return queues late afternoon).
- What to expect: ~10–12 km walking incl. steps; sun hat/rain jacket (NE coast is wet Oct–Mar); grippy shoes; cash for snacks. Budget per person ≈ NT$700–1,000 (bus ~NT$105 each way + food + museum entry). Estimate.

**Plan H2 "Volcano & sulfur": Yangmingshan + Beitou + Elephant Mtn optional** 
1. 07:30 MRT to Jiantan; R5 or 260 to Yangmingshan Bus Terminal.
2. 09:00 Bus 108 → Xiaoyoukeng (fumaroles; optional Mt Qixing summit hike, 2–3 h — unverified duration) → 108 to Qingtiangang (45-min loop on grassland).
3. 12:30 Lunch at Yangmingshan terminal area; bus down to Beitou (route numbers vary — unverified; e.g., 230 is often cited but NOT verified here).
4. 14:00–16:30 Beitou walking route (Xinbeitou Station → Beitou Park → Library → Hot Spring Museum → Thermal Valley), soak at a hotel day-bath (Millennium is closed).
5. 17:30 Either MRT Red Line to Tamsui for sunset, OR cross town to Xiangshan for Elephant Mtn night view (tight).
- What to expect: Mon = Museum + Thermal Valley closed. Layers (mountain is several °C cooler, windy, often foggy). Budget ≈ NT$600–1,500 depending on bath choice (estimate).

**Plan H3 (seasonal) Caoling Historic Trail** — TRA to Fulong (beach + bento) → hike 8.5 km to Dali → TRA back; Nov–Dec silvergrass. Train times not verified; full day.

## Q2. Shopping (day plans)

### Takeaway
A full shopping day can chain Dihua Street (morning, traditional goods) → Chifeng/Zhongshan boutiques (from 11:00) → Huashan design shops → Guanghua electronics → Wufenpu (afternoon/evening; opens ~13:00) → Raohe night market, or a mall/outlet variant (Xinyi malls + Mitsui Outlet Linkou via Airport MRT).

### Cited Findings
- Wufenpu: garment wholesale district, Xinyi District; stalls in alleys off Yongji Road; best early afternoon–evening; most stores ~13:30–23:00 (other sources: 13:00–22:00; ~11:00–21:00; Sat later, Sun to 21:00) — conflicting hours; minimum purchases at some stalls; carry cash; avoid Monday — [travel.taipei Wufenpu](https://www.travel.taipei/en/attraction/details/2101); [Taipei City Govt](https://english.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=E2EAD3BDAD47F41F&sms=1FB5FD7A43084A6C&s=15C88D946D06469C); [Taiwan Obsessed shopping, snippet](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/shopping-in-taipei/).
- Wufenpu access: MRT Houshanpi Station (Bannan/Blue Line) Exit 1, walk to Zhongpo N. Rd / Yongji Rd intersection — [TravelKing](https://www.travelking.com.tw/eng/tourguide/taipei/taipei-wufengpu-shopping-district.html); [travel.taipei](https://www.travel.taipei/en/attraction/details/2101). (Some sources also say "near Songshan MRT"; Raohe/Songshan is walkable.)
- Chifeng Street (Datong Dist.): vintage/thrift, independent cafés, retro accessories, vinyl; most shops ~11:00–21:00 — [Taiwan Obsessed shopping, snippet](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/shopping-in-taipei/).
- Guanghua Digital Plaza + adjacent Syntrend: best place for electronics; ~10:00–21:00 daily (stalls vary); 6 floors; short walk from MRT Zhongxiao Xinsheng — [Eager Nomad](https://eagernomad.com/guanghua-digital-plaza/); [Taipei Travel Geek](https://www.taipeitravelgeek.com/taipei-technology-district); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guang_Hua_Digital_Plaza).
- Dihua Street: best-preserved Qing-era commercial street; fabric, TCM, dried goods; Yongle Market for floral cloth; ~09:00–19:00; walk from MRT Beimen or Daqiaotou — [Ms Travel Solo](https://www.mstravelsolo.com/dihua-street-dadaocheng-taipei-taiwan/); [Foreigners in Taiwan](https://www.foreignersintaiwan.com/blog/dihua-street-and-dadaocheng).
- Mitsui Outlet Park Linkou: ~300 brands, open-air; Mon–Thu 11:00–21:30, Fri 11:00–22:00, Sat–Sun 10:30–22:00; Taoyuan Airport MRT A1 Taipei Main → A9 Linkou (~20 min) + free shuttle — [Mitsui official access page](https://www.mitsui-shopping-park.com.tw/mop/linkou/en/access.html); [search summary / Agoda](https://www.agoda.com/travel-guides/taiwan/taipei/shop-taipei-outlet-mall-your-guide-to-sales-savings/). Note: A9 is on the Airport MRT, not the Bannan Line (one aggregator snippet mislabeled it).
- SunnyHills (Minsheng Park): free pineapple cake + oolong tea on arrival; ~10:00–18:00 (some list to 20:00); MRT Taipei Arena Exit 5 ~15 min walk — [Tablejourney](https://tablejourney.com/taiwan/taipei/bakeries/sunnyhills-taipei-minsheng/); [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g13806900-d4367880-Reviews-Sunnyhills-Songshan_Taipei.html).
- Chia Te Bakery: No. 88, Sec. 5, Nanjing E. Rd; next to MRT Nanjing Sanmin; 08:30–20:30; queues 45+ min — [Chloe's Travelogue](https://chloestravelogue.com/best-pineapple-cake-in-taipei-taiwan-chiate-sunnyhills-leechitaipei/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chia_Te_Bakery); [Eating in Taipei 2026](https://eatingintaipei.com/pineapple-cake-taipei/).

### Inferences — Day plans
**Plan S1 "Local & indie" (Datong/Zhongshan → Zhongzheng → Songshan)**
1. 09:30 Dihua Street + Yongle Market (dried fruit, teas, floral cloth); MRT Beimen. Breakfast/brunch in Dadaocheng.
2. 11:30 Walk/MRT to Zhongshan: Chifeng Street vintage + Zhongshan boutiques (shops open ~11:00).
3. 13:30 Lunch Zhongshan.
4. 14:30 Huashan 1914 Creative Park design shops (MRT Zhongxiao Xinsheng) → Guanghua Digital Plaza next door area.
5. 16:30 MRT to Nanjing Sanmin: Chia Te pineapple cakes (queue).
6. 17:30 Wufenpu (MRT Houshanpi Exit 1) — evening stalls.
7. 19:00 Walk to Raohe Night Market (dinner + cheap goods). 
- Expect: 12k–15k steps; cash essential at Wufenpu/Dihua/night markets; carry tote. Budget excl. purchases ≈ NT$500–800 food/transport.

**Plan S2 "Malls & outlets"**: 10:30 Mitsui Outlet Linkou (Airport MRT A9 + shuttle) → 14:30 back to Taipei Main → Xinyi District malls (Taipei 101 mall, ATT 4 FUN, Breeze/Shin Kong Mitsukoshi — hours not verified) → Eslite → night at Raohe or Xinyi rooftop. Card-friendly; budget depends on purchases.

## Q4. Sightseeing classics (first-timer highlights)

### Takeaway
Taiwan Obsessed's 1-day plan: early Taiwanese breakfast (Fu Hang Soy Milk, opens 05:30, closed Mon) → Longshan Temple → CKS Memorial Hall (MRT Blue Line to Ximen, transfer Green Line two stops) → ... → night market; Taipei 101 observatory NT$600 (88/89/91F), NT$980 with Skyline 460 (101F).
